## 8. WHAT MAKES A WINNING SOLUTION

Based on research from similar sustainability hackathons:

### Key Success Factors
1. **Problem-Solution Fit** - Clear problem statement with a well-defined solution
2. **Real-World Impact** - Measurable environmental/social benefit
3. **Scalability** - Can be replicated in other regions/contexts
4. **Technology Integration** - Smart use of AI, IoT, blockchain, etc.
5. **Prototype Working Demo** - Functional proof of concept
6. **Team Diversity** - Different skills (tech, design, domain expertise)
7. **Clear Pitch** - 3-5 minute explanation that everyone understands

### Common Winning Themes
- **Climate-smart agriculture** with IoT sensors
- **Waste management** using AI-powered sorting
- **Water quality monitoring** with real-time data
- **Renewable energy optimization**
- **Carbon footprint tracking** apps
- **Sustainable supply chain** solutions
