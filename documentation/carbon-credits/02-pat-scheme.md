# Perform, Achieve and Trade (PAT) Scheme

- **Source:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power
- **Version:** Cycle VII (2022-25)
- **Year:** 2012-2026
- **URL:** https://beeindia.gov.in/en/programmesnmeee/pat
- **Type:** Policy / Scheme Document
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Provides precedent for India's energy credit trading mechanism and demonstrates market-based approaches to emission reductions that can inform agricultural carbon credit frameworks.

## Summary
PAT is a regulatory instrument to reduce Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) in energy-intensive industries, with an associated market-based mechanism through certification of excess energy savings (ESCerts) that can be traded. Launched in 2012 under the National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency (NMEEE).

## Key Points
- PAT Cycle-I (2012-15): 478 DCs in 8 sectors, saved 8.67 MTOE (31 million tonne CO2 avoided)
- PAT Cycle-II (2016-19): 621 DCs in 11 sectors, saved 14.08 MTOE (68 million tonne CO2 avoided)
- PAT Cycle-III (2017-20): 116 DCs, saved 1.594 MTOE (5.59 million tonne CO2 avoided)
- PAT Cycle-VII (2022-25): 707 DCs with 8.485 MTOE target
- Total 1,434 DCs across all cycles
- 1 ESCert = 1 tonne of Oil Equivalent (toe) = INR 10,968
- ESCerts tradable on Indian Energy Exchange and Power Exchange India

## Technical Details
### PAT Mechanism
1. **Baseline Year:** SEC measured for each Designated Consumer (DC)
2. **Target Year:** SEC reduction targets notified (based on current efficiency levels)
3. **Monitoring & Verification:** Accredited energy auditors verify SEC
4. **ESCert Issuance:** DCs exceeding targets receive ESCerts for excess savings
5. **Trading:** DCs failing targets can purchase ESCerts from power exchanges
6. **Compliance:** 3-year cycles with penalty for non-compliance

### Key Sectors Covered
- Thermal Power Plants
- Iron & Steel
- Cement
- Fertilizer
- Aluminum
- Textile
- Pulp & Paper
- Chlor-Alkali
- Refineries
- Railways
- DISCOMs
- Commercial Buildings (Hotels)
- Petrochemicals

### ESCert Trading
- Trading commenced September 2017
- Total ~12.98 lakh ESCerts traded
- Business value ~INR 100 crores
- ESCerts valid until compliance period of next cycle
- Can be banked for future cycles
