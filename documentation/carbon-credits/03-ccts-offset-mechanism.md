# CCTS Offset Mechanism Detailed Procedures

- **Source:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Year:** 2024-2025
- **URL:** https://beeindia.gov.in/
- **Type:** Methodology / Procedure Document
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Defines how agricultural carbon credit projects (including rice paddy methane reduction) can register under India's Offset Mechanism to earn Carbon Credit Certificates.

## Summary
The Offset Mechanism under CCTS allows non-obligated entities to register projects that lead to GHG emission reductions, avoidance, or removals for issuance of Carbon Credit Certificates (CCC). The mechanism follows core values aligned with global frameworks ensuring high integrity of carbon credits.

## Key Points
- BEE is Administrator for Offset Mechanism
- Project start date: January 1, 2025 (no retrospective projects)
- Must fall within 10 sectors published by Central Government
- Cannot be concurrently registered with other carbon markets (except Green Credit Programme)
- No double-counting allowed
- Must demonstrate no significant harm to environment
- Projects mandated by law not eligible (must be additional to business-as-usual)

## Technical Details
### Core Values (Aligned with Global Frameworks)
1. Robust and Transparent governance structure and process
2. Effective third-party validation and verification
3. Strong focus on Additionality and Permanence
4. Robust quantification of emission reductions and removals
5. No double-counting
6. Alignment with UN SDGs

### Project Standard Requirements
- **Governance:** Clear accountability, no double counting, effective third-party validation
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Must adhere to all national, state, local regulations
- **Project Start Date:** January 1, 2025 or later
- **Sectoral Scope:** Must fall within 10 published sectors
- **Exclusivity:** Cannot be registered with other carbon markets
- **Preventing Double Counting:** Entity must ensure no double counting
- **No Significant Harm:** Must follow SDG Framework guidelines
- **Regulatory Surplus:** Must be additional to business-as-usual

### Validation and Verification
- Accredited Carbon Verification Agencies (ACVAs) assess projects
- Third-party validation and verification required
- High levels of transparency and accountability

### SDG Integration
- Environmental and social impact analysis required
- Must identify possible negative impacts and mitigation plans
- Must quantify positive impact on SDGs

### Ten Sectors for Offset Projects
1. Energy
2. Energy distribution
3. Energy demand
4. Manufacturing
5. Chemical industry
6. Transport
7. Mining and mineral production
8. Metal production
9. Waste handling and disposal
10. Agriculture, Forestry, and Other Land Use (AFOLU)
