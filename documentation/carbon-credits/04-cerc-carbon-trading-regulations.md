# CERC Carbon Credit Certificate Trading Regulations 2024

- **Source:** Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC)
- **Version:** Draft 2024
- **Year:** 2024
- **URL:** https://www.cercind.gov.in/
- **Type:** Regulation / Legal Framework
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Defines the trading framework for Carbon Credit Certificates on power exchanges, establishing how agricultural carbon credits can be monetized.

## Summary
CERC has been designated as the market regulator for Carbon Credit trading under CCTS 2023. The regulations define the structure and framework for exchange of CCCs on Power Exchanges, with separate market segments for Compliance Market (obligated entities) and Offset Market (non-obligated entities).

## Key Points
- CERC is regulator for trading activities under Indian Carbon Market
- Grid Controller of India functions as Registry for CCC exchange
- Two separate market segments: Compliance Market and Offset Market
- CCCs shall be dealt with only through Power Exchange (not any other manner)
- Monthly trading frequency (or as per Commission procedure)
- Floor price and forbearance price set by Commission on Bureau recommendation
- Entities must register with Power Exchange to participate

## Technical Details
### Market Structure
1. **Compliance Market:** For obligated entities meeting GHG emission intensity targets
2. **Offset Market:** For non-obligated entities with registered offset projects
3. **Independence:** Compliance and Offset markets are independent of each other

### Trading Rules
- All CCCs dealt through Power Exchange only
- Monthly trading frequency (or as per Commission approval)
- Entities register with Power Exchange
- CCCs placed on any Power Exchange as holder considers appropriate
- Sale bids cannot exceed total CCCs held in Registry Account
- Registry cross-checks cumulative sale bids with available CCCs

### Pricing
- 1 CCC = 1 tCO2e reduction/removal/avoidance
- Market price discovered through bidding at Power Exchange
- CCCs exchanged within floor price and forbearance price
- Floor and forbearance price approved by Commission on Bureau proposal

### Default and Penalties
- Entities with >3 default cases in quarter barred from dealing for 6 months
- Defaulting entity's bids become void and ineffective
- List of defaulting entities published monthly by Registry

### Bureau Functions
- Formulate detailed procedures after public consultation
- Register eligible entities on Registry
- Manage CCCs including transfers
- Coordinate with Power Exchanges and Registry
- Provide entity information and market information
