# Gold Standard Water Benefit Certificate Methodology

- **Source:** Gold Standard Foundation
- **Year:** 2014-2024
- **URL:** https://globalgoals.goldstandard.org/standards/PRE-GS4GG-Water/wbs-requirements-document_161115.pdf
- **Type:** Methodology Framework
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Provides the certification framework for water credits that AGRI-GRID can use to monetize water savings from AWD rice cultivation.

## Summary

The Gold Standard Water Benefit Standard (WBS) is a results-based financing mechanism for water projects. Launched in 2014, it implements a "payment for performance" approach similar to carbon credits but focused on water benefits.

### Core Concept
- **1 Water Benefit Certificate (WBC)** = defined volume of water sustainably supplied, purified, and/or conserved
- Each WBC represents measurable water impact verified by independent third parties
- Creates annuity revenue streams for water projects through credit purchases

### Framework Structure
1. **Project Registration:** Water projects register under Gold Standard criteria
2. **Impact Quantification:** Methodologies measure water benefits (volume saved/improved)
3. **Verification:** Independent monitoring, reporting, and verification (MRV)
4. **Credit Issuance:** WBCs issued proportional to verified water benefits
5. **Trading:** Companies purchase WBCs to offset water usage/stewardship goals

## Key Findings

### Certification Requirements
- Robust accounting of emission reductions
- Strict safeguards for human rights and local communities
- Requirements for gender equality and women's empowerment
- Clear contribution to multiple Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

### WBC Characteristics
- Represents **1,000 m³** of water sustainably managed (preliminary standard)
- Quality parameters defined based on host country regulations
- Sector-specific requirements (industrial, agricultural, municipal)

### Market Application
- Companies purchase WBCs to meet water stewardship goals
- Enables offsetting of water consumption through project investment
- Pilot projects operational in Uganda and Ghana
- Target: 200 Water Benefit projects by 2025, benefitting 3+ million people

### Gold Standard Integration
- WBCs tracked in dedicated Water Benefits Registry
- Future integration into Impact Registry for unified tracking
- Projects must demonstrate co-benefits beyond water (climate, gender, livelihoods)

## AGRI-GRID Citation

The Gold Standard WBS methodology provides the certification pathway for AGRI-GRID's water savings from AWD rice cultivation. By registering AWD projects under this framework, AGRI-GRID can generate Water Benefit Certificates alongside carbon credits, creating dual revenue streams for participating farmers. The 15% water savings from AWD can be quantified and certified as WBCs.
