# Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) 2023

- **Source:** Ministry of Power / Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)
- **Version:** 2023 (S.O. 2825(E))
- **Year:** 2023
- **URL:** https://beeindia.gov.in/
- **Type:** Policy / Scheme Document
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Establishes the Indian carbon market framework where AGRI-GRID's agricultural carbon credits can be traded as Carbon Credit Certificates (CCC).

## Summary
The Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) 2023 was notified by the Ministry of Power under Section 14(w) of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001. It establishes the Indian Carbon Market (ICM) with a national framework for trading Carbon Credit Certificates (CCC) to reduce, remove, or avoid greenhouse gas emissions.

## Key Points
- CCTS notified vide S.O. 2825(E) dated 28th June 2023
- Two mechanisms: Compliance Mechanism and Offset Mechanism
- Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) is the Administrator
- Grid Controller of India Limited is the Registry
- Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) is the Market Regulator
- 1 CCC = 1 tonne of CO2 equivalent (tCO2e) reduction/removal/avoidance
- Compliance Market: obligated entities must meet GHG emission intensity targets
- Offset Market: non-obligated entities can register projects for CCC issuance

## Technical Details
### Governance Structure
1. **National Steering Committee for Indian Carbon Market:** Direct oversight, recommends targets and procedures
2. **Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE):** Administrator - identifies sectors, issues CCCs, develops procedures
3. **Grid Controller of India Limited:** Registry - manages CCC accounts and transfers
4. **CERC:** Market Regulator - regulates trading, safeguards interests, prevents fraud

### Compliance Mechanism
- Sectors and obligated entities decided by Ministry of Power based on BEE recommendations
- GHG emission intensity targets in tCO2e per unit of equivalent product
- 3-year trajectory periods with annual compliance cycles
- Entities exceeding targets receive CCCs; those falling short must purchase CCCs
- Non-compliance: Environmental Compensation = 2x average CCC trading price

### Offset Mechanism
- Non-obligated entities can register projects leading to GHG emission reductions
- BEE registers projects and issues CCCs
- Project start date: January 1, 2025 (no retrospective projects)
- Must align with 10 sectors published by Central Government
- Cannot be concurrently registered with other carbon markets (except Green Credit Programme)
