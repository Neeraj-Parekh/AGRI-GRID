## Key Concepts for AGRI-GRID

### Carbon Credit Certificate (CCC)
- 1 CCC = 1 tonne CO2 equivalent (tCO2e) reduction/removal/avoidance
- Tradeable on Indian Energy Exchange and Power Exchange India
- Two market segments: Compliance Market and Offset Market

### Offset Mechanism for Agriculture
- Non-obligated entities (including farmers/FPOs) can register projects
- Must fall within 10 sectors (including Agriculture, Forestry, and Other Land Use)
- Project start date: January 1, 2025 or later
- Cannot be concurrently registered with other carbon markets
- Must demonstrate additionality and no double-counting

### Agricultural Carbon Credit Methodologies
- Rice paddy methane reduction (alternate wetting and drying)
- Soil carbon sequestration
- Reduced fertilizer use
- Agroforestry
- Improved crop residue management

### Trading Framework
- CERC is market regulator
- Grid Controller of India is Registry
- BEE is Administrator
- Trading on Power Exchanges only
- Floor price and forbearance price set by Commission

### Compliance for Obligated Entities
- GHG emission intensity targets in tCO2e per unit product
- 3-year trajectory periods with annual compliance cycles
- Entities exceeding targets receive CCCs
- Entities falling short must purchase CCCs
- Non-compliance: Environmental Compensation = 2x average CCC price
