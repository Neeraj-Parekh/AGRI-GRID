# IRRI India AWD Field Trials and Implementation

- **Source:** International Rice Research Institute (IRRI)
- **Year:** 2023-2024
- **URL:** https://www.irri.org/where-we-work/india
- **Type:** Field Trial Report
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Provides India-specific evidence for AWD adoption including water savings of 25-30% and methane reduction of 48% in Haryana, Punjab, and Western UP contexts.

## Summary

IRRI has conducted extensive field trials and capacity building workshops across India to promote Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) technology. The program targets water-stressed states including Haryana, Punjab, and Western Uttar Pradesh where groundwater depletion is critical.

### Key Trial Sites
- **Haryana:** Workshops conducted in Yamuna Nagar, Karnal, Panipat, and Sonipat districts (June 2024)
- **Punjab:** Training programs focusing on water-saving irrigation techniques
- **Eastern India (Bihar, Odisha, Jharkhand):** ICAR-IRRI collaborative trials

### Technical Implementation
- AWD technology developed by IRRI saves up to **30% water** compared to conventional flooding
- Promotes good root anchorage and reduces methane emissions by **48%**
- Field water tube (pani pipe) method for monitoring water levels
- Water level threshold: 15 cm below soil surface before re-irrigation

## Key Findings

- **Water Savings:** 25-30% reduction in irrigation water use without yield penalty
- **Methane Reduction:** 48% reduction in CH₄ emissions from rice paddies
- **Groundwater Impact:** Between 2000-2021, groundwater levels in Haryana rice districts dipped 13 meters
- **Economic Viability:** Low-cost training solution compared to alternatives like land leveling or electricity subsidy reduction
- **Adoption Challenges:** Farmers need ownership of pumps and awareness of water scarcity for adoption

## AGRI-GRID Citation

IRRI India trials provide ground-truth data for AGRI-GRID's carbon credit validation in Indian rice systems. The 25-30% water savings and 48% methane reduction figures form the basis of our emissions reduction calculations. Workshops in Haryana demonstrate scalable farmer training models for AGRI-GRID deployment.
