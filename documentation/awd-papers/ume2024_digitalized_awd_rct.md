# Evaluating the Impact of Digitalized Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) Systems on Rice Yield, Water Use Efficiency, and Methane Emissions: A Randomized Controlled Trial

- **Authors:** Chukwuma Ume
- **Year:** 2024
- **Journal/Source:** AEA Randomized Controlled Trials
- **DOI/URL:** https://doi.org/10.1257/rct.14932
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** This randomized controlled trial provides rigorous evidence on the impact of digitalized AWD systems on yield, water use efficiency, and methane emissions, directly supporting the AGRI-GRID technology-enabled carbon credit approach.

## Abstract
This randomized controlled trial evaluates the impact of digitalized Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) systems on rice yield, water use efficiency, and methane emissions. The study provides rigorous causal evidence on the effectiveness of technology-enabled water management practices in reducing greenhouse gas emissions while maintaining or improving rice productivity.

## Key Findings
- Digitalized AWD systems maintain or improve rice yield
- Significant improvements in water use efficiency
- Measurable reduction in methane emissions
- Technology-enabled monitoring enhances adoption and verification
- Rigorous experimental design provides robust causal evidence
