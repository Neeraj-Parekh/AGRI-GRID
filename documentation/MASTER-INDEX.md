## 🔑 Key Findings (from downloaded data)

### AWD → Methane Reduction
- AWD reduces CH₄ by **34–57.5%** (Hoang 2023, Chu 2015, Xuan 2025)
- Water savings of **27.6–50%** documented
- Rice yield **maintained or increased** (4.4–18% with AWD)
- Global warming potential reduced by **45.2–62.6%**

### India Agriculture
- Rice: **51,267K ha**, **150.2 MMT**, yield 2,929 kg/ha
- India = **6.5 Tg CH₄/yr** from rice (2nd globally)
- Agriculture uses **76–78%** of India's water
- Irrigation efficiency: only **35–40%** (target: 60%)
- **146 BCM** savings potential with efficient methods

### Agri Stack
- **8.48+ crore** farmer IDs registered
- UFSI provides standardized REST APIs
- APIs are production-ready with OAuth 2.0

### Carbon Credits
- CCTS 2023 covers **10 sectors** including AFOLU
- Offset mechanism allows agricultural projects
- PAT scheme: 7 cycles, 1,434 DCs, ESCert trading

### LoRa Technical
- SX1262: **170 dB** link budget, **-134 dBm** sensitivity
- IN865: India **865–867 MHz**, 30 dBm EIRP
- Range: **10–15 km** open field, **3–5 km** dense crops
- Battery life: **3+ years** at 15-min intervals
