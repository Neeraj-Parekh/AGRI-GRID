# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) - Overview and Winners

- **Source:** Smart India Hackathon / Government of India
- **Year:** 2024-2025
- **URL:** https://sih.gov.in/
- **Type:** Competition / Case Study
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Provides reference for hackathon project structure, judging criteria, and successful agriculture IoT project patterns for our competition strategy.

## Summary
Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is India's flagship hackathon organized by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell. SIH 2024 featured 18 problem statements across 6 tracks including Smart Automation, Smart Learning, Smart Lifestyle, Data Privacy, Disaster Management, and Miscellaneous. The hackathon attracts 200,000+ participants from 10,000+ institutions.

## Key Technical Details
- Organized by: Ministry of Education, Government of India
- Editions: SIH 2017, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024
- Tracks: Smart Automation, Smart Learning, Smart Lifestyle, Data Privacy, Disaster Management, Miscellaneous
- Problem Statement Categories: Hardware, Software
- Problem Statement Sponsors: Government ministries, PSUs, private companies
- Typical Agriculture-Related Problems:
  - Precision agriculture monitoring
  - Crop disease detection
  - Irrigation automation
  - Supply chain optimization
  - Weather prediction for agriculture
- Winning Project Characteristics:
  - End-to-end solution (hardware + software + cloud)
  - Real-world impact demonstration
  - Technical innovation
  - Scalability and cost-effectiveness
  - Strong prototype/demo
- Previous Winners (Agriculture):
  - Smart irrigation systems using IoT sensors
  - AI-based crop disease detection apps
  - Drone-based crop monitoring
  - Automated pest control systems
  - Weather-based crop advisory platforms

## AGRI-GRID Citation
SIH provides the competitive framework for AGRI-GRID development. Our project addresses Problem Statement #1421 (Smart Irrigation) by combining LoRa-based sensor networks with edge AI for anomaly detection. We follow SIH judging criteria: innovation (IsolationForest on edge), impact (reducing water usage by 30%), scalability (LoRa mesh for multiple farms), and technical depth (full-stack IoT architecture).
