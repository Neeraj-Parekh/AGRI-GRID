## Key Statistics Summary

### India Rice Production (2024-25)
- Total production: 150.18 MMT (record)
- Area: 51,266.60 thousand hectares
- Yield: 2,929 kg/hectare
- 9th consecutive record harvest
- 5-year CAGR: Area 3.0%, Production 4.6%, Yield 1.64%

### Water Use
- Agriculture: 76-78% of India's total water use
- Groundwater: 62% of irrigation
- Current irrigation efficiency: 35-40%
- Potential savings with drip/sprinkler: 146 BCM/year
- Total demand projection: 634 BCM (2000) → 1,447 BCM (2050)

### Methane Emissions
- India rice methane: 6.5 ± 1.0 Tg a⁻¹ (2022)
- Global rice methane: 39.3 ± 4.7 Tg a⁻¹
- India = 2nd largest rice methane emitter
- Agriculture = 74% of India's CH₄ emissions
- Rice = 21% of agricultural methane

## Usage Notes
- Data sourced from official Indian government, USDA, and peer-reviewed scientific publications
- Cross-reference production data between DA&FW (#01) and USDA (#05) for validation
- Methane data from multiple independent studies provides uncertainty bounds
- All statistics are verifiable through cited URLs
