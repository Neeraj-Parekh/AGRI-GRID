# Economic Survey 2024-25: Agriculture Chapter

- **Source:** Ministry of Finance, Government of India
- **Year:** 2025-26
- **URL:** https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2248987
- **Type:** Official Government Report
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Provides official statistics on rice production, water use in agriculture, and policy direction for sustainable farming that validates AGRI-GRID's approach.

## Summary

The Economic Survey 2024-25 highlights India's agricultural resilience with record foodgrain production of 357.73 MMT. The agriculture chapter addresses water sustainability, methane emissions from rice, and the need for crop-neutral incentive structures.

### Key Agricultural Statistics (2024-25)

| Crop | Production | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| Rice | 150.18 MMT | Record, 2nd largest globally |
| Wheat | 117.94 MMT | Record |
| Foodgrains | 357.73 MMT | 25.43 MMT increase over previous year |
| Horticulture | 362.08 MT | Structural shift to high-value crops |

### Rice Production by State
- **Uttar Pradesh:** 20.76 MT (13.82% share)
- **Telangana:** 17.45 MT (11.62% share)
- **West Bengal:** 16.02 MT (10.66% share)

## Key Findings

### Water and Agriculture
- Agriculture accounts for **nearly one-fifth** of GVA
- Employs **46.1% of workforce**, supports 55% population
- Average annual growth rate: **4.4%** at constant prices (last 5 years)

### Water Sustainability Issues
- **Irrigation Efficiency:** Only 30-40% for surface water, 50-60% for groundwater
- **Total Net Irrigated Area:** Needs expansion; several states below national average
- **Water-Guzzling Crops:** Rice and sugarcane specifically identified as problematic

### Methane Emissions
- Rice cultivation identified as source of methane emissions
- Survey recommends **crop-neutral incentive structures**
- Need for technologies like drip irrigation and fertigation

### Policy Recommendations
1. Export bans only under exceptional circumstances
2. Re-examine inflation-targeting framework (target inflation excluding food)
3. Increase Total Net Irrigated Area
4. Better water utilisation through drip and fertigation
5. Make farming consistent with climate considerations

### Agricultural Exports
- FY20: USD 34.5 billion → FY25: USD 51.1 billion (CAGR 8.2%)
- Processed food share increased from 14.9% (FY18) to 20.4% (FY25)
- Rice exports: USD 12.95 billion in 2024-25

### Credit and Investment
- Agricultural credit disbursement: Rs. 28.67 lakh crore in FY 2024-25
- PM-KISAN: Rs. 4.27 lakh crore disbursed in 22 instalments
- Budget allocation: Rs. 21,933 crore (2013-14) → Rs. 1.30 lakh crore (2026-27)

## AGRI-GRID Citation

The Economic Survey validates AGRI-GRID's focus on rice methane reduction. The official recognition of rice as a "water-guzzling" crop with methane emissions supports policy alignment. The survey's recommendation for "crop-neutral incentive structures" provides governmental backing for carbon credit mechanisms in rice cultivation. The 150.18 MMT rice production figure represents AGRI-GRID's addressable market for AWD deployment.
