# USDA Grain and Feed Update: India Rice Production 2024-25

- **Source:** USDA Foreign Agricultural Service (FAS), New Delhi
- **Year:** 2025 (report date: November 20, 2025)

- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Independent international verification of India's rice production data with area, production, and yield figures for crop year analysis.

## Summary
USDA FAS confirms India's record grain production of 357.7 MMT for 2024/25 (July-June), the ninth consecutive record harvest. Rice production at 150.2 MMT from 51.3 million hectares. Post forecasts 2025/26 rice production at 151 MMT from 51.5 million hectares (tenth consecutive record). Kharif rice planting estimated at 44.2 million hectares. Above normal 2025 monsoon supports upcoming harvest. Adequate rains replenished surface and groundwater for rabi rice irrigation.

## Key Statistics
- India rice production 2024/25: 150.2 MMT (record) from 51.3 M ha
- Previous year: 137.8 MMT (2023/24)
- Forecast 2025/26: 151 MMT from 51.5 M ha
- Kharif rice planting 2025: 44.2 million hectares
- Total food grain 2024/25: 357.7 MMT (record)
- Wheat 2024/25: 117.95 MMT (record)
- Maize 2024/25: 43.4 MMT (record)
- Area harvested rice: 7,828 (1000 ha) reference
