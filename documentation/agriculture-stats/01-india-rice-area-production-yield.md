# India Rice Area, Production, and Yield Statistics (1990-2025)

- **Source:** Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DA&FW), Government of India
- **Year:** 2024-25 (data series 1990-91 to 2024-25)
- **URL:** https://dataful.in/datasets/21404/
- **Type:** Official Statistics / Dataset
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Comprehensive state-wise area, production and yield data for rice across 35 years, essential for AGRI-GRID's crop modeling and water footprint calculations.

## Summary
Official state-wise data on area (thousand hectares), production (thousand tonnes), and yield (kg/hectare) for rice in India from 1990-91 to 2024-25. The dataset covers all 32 states/UTs and provides season-wise breakdown (Kharif, Rabi, Summer). The data shows consistent growth in production driven by yield improvements rather than area expansion.

## Key Statistics
- All India rice area 2024-25: 51,266.60 thousand hectares
- All India rice production 2024-25: 150,184.30 thousand tonnes (150.18 MMT)
- All India rice yield 2024-25: 2,929 kg/hectare
- Top producing states 2024-25:
  - Uttar Pradesh: 20.76 MMT
  - Telangana: 17.45 MMT
  - West Bengal: 16.02 MMT
  - Punjab: 17.99 MMT
  - Bihar: 8.30 MMT
- 5-year CAGR (2020-21 to 2024-25): Area 3.0%, Production 4.6%, Yield 1.64%
