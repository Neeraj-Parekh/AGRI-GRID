# Greenhouse gas emission from rice fields: a review from Indian context

- **Authors:** K. Gupta, R. Kumar, K.K. Baruah, S. Hazarika, S. Karmakar, N. Bordoloi
- **Year:** 2021
- **Journal/Source:** Environmental Science and Pollution Research
- **DOI/URL:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-021-13935-1
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** This review provides India-specific data on greenhouse gas emissions from rice fields, supporting the applicability of AWD-based carbon credits in the Indian context.

## Abstract
Rice cultivation in India is a significant source of greenhouse gas emissions, particularly methane. This review synthesizes research on greenhouse gas emissions from Indian rice fields, examining the factors influencing emissions and mitigation strategies. The paper provides context-specific data for developing carbon credit methodologies in Indian rice cultivation systems.

## Key Findings
- Indian rice fields are significant sources of methane emissions
- Water management practices influence emission levels
- Local conditions affect the effectiveness of mitigation strategies
- India-specific data is crucial for developing relevant carbon credit methodologies
- Mitigation potential exists through improved water management
