# Effects of Water and Fertilizer Management Practices on Methane Emissions from Paddy Soils: Synthesis and Perspective

- **Authors:** X. Gu, S. Weng, Y. Li, X. Zhou
- **Year:** 2022
- **Journal/Source:** International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health
- **DOI/URL:** https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph19127324
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** This synthesis examines how water and fertilizer management practices affect methane emissions from paddy soils, providing evidence for optimizing AWD practices for maximum emission reductions.

## Abstract
This paper synthesizes research on the effects of water and fertilizer management practices on methane emissions from paddy soils. The study examines how different irrigation regimes and fertilizer applications influence methane production and emission rates, providing insights for optimizing mitigation strategies in rice cultivation systems.

## Key Findings
- Water management practices significantly influence methane emissions
- AWD reduces methane emissions compared to continuous flooding
- Fertilizer type and application timing affect emission rates
- Integrated management approaches optimize emission reductions
- Local conditions modify the effectiveness of mitigation strategies
