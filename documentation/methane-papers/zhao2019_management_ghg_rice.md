# Management-induced greenhouse gases emission mitigation in global rice production

- **Authors:** X. Zhao, C. Pu, S.T. Ma, S.L. Liu, J.F. Xue, X. Wang, Y.Q. Wang, S.S. Li, R. Lal, F. Chen, H.L. Zhang
- **Year:** 2019
- **Journal/Source:** Science of the Total Environment
- **DOI/URL:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2018.08.392
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** This global meta-analysis quantifies the mitigation potential of management practices in rice production, providing robust evidence for the effectiveness of AWD in reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

## Abstract
This study presents a global meta-analysis of management-induced greenhouse gas emission mitigation in rice production. The research quantifies the effectiveness of various management practices, including water management, fertilizer application, and residue management, in reducing greenhouse gas emissions from rice cultivation systems worldwide.

## Key Findings
- Water management practices, particularly AWD, show significant mitigation potential
- Global analysis provides robust evidence for emission reduction strategies
- Management practices can reduce greenhouse gas emissions while maintaining yield
- Region-specific optimization enhances mitigation effectiveness
- Integrated approaches combining multiple practices yield greatest reductions
