# Final Estimate of Production of Food Grains for 2024-25

- **Source:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DA&FW), Government of India
- **Year:** 2025 (released 20 November 2025)
- **URL:** https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251120700901.pdf
- **Type:** Official Government Statistics
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Provides authoritative final production estimates for rice and other food grains in India for 2024-25, essential for baseline crop data and water footprint calculations.

## Summary
The DA&FW released the final production estimates for major food grains for the Indian Crop Year 2024-25 (July-June). India achieved a record total food grain production of 357.73 million metric tons (MMT), the ninth consecutive record harvest. Rice production reached an all-time high of 150.18 MMT, surpassing the previous record of 137.83 MMT in 2023-24. The gains were driven by normal 2024 monsoon rains, timely planting, and absence of major pest or weather disruptions.

## Key Statistics
- Total food grain production 2024-25: 357.73 MMT (up from 332.30 MMT in 2023-24, +7.6%)
- Rice production 2024-25: 150.18 MMT (record, up from 137.83 MMT, +9.0%)
- Wheat production 2024-25: 117.95 MMT (record, up from 113.29 MMT)
- Maize production 2024-25: 43.40 MMT (record, up from 37.67 MMT)
- Total oilseeds 2024-25: 43.0 MMT (up from 39.7 MMT)
- Sugarcane 2024-25: 454.6 MMT
