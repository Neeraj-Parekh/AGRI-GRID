- **Source:** Kubernein Initiative (with financial support from Hinduja Foundation)

- **URL:** https://kuberneininitiative.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Kubernein-Initiative_WaterReport.pdf
- **Type:** Policy Research Report
- **Relevance to AGRI-GRID:** Provides traditional water management practices mapped across India's 10 biogeographic zones with 5 priority areas including Water-Energy-Food Nexus, directly applicable to AGRI-GRID's regional water strategy.

## Summary
India supports 18% of world population with only 4.3% of freshwater resources. Annual precipitation is ~4,000 BCM but only ~25% is utilizable. Agriculture accounts for ~90% of 761,000 billion liters of annual freshwater withdrawals. Per capita water consumption in agriculture ranges from 4,913 to 5,800 kiloliters per capita per year. The report maps traditional water management across 10 biogeographic zones and identifies 5 priority areas: Gender Equity, Community & Livelihood Development, Water-Energy-Food Nexus, Climate Risk & Resilience, and Groundwater & Aquifer Recharge. It advocates transitioning from political to hydrological boundaries for watershed management.

## Key Statistics
- India has 18% of world population with only 4.3% of freshwater

- 10 biogeographic zones mapped for water management practices
- 5 priority areas identified for intersectional water management
